# 04 — Procedural Generation: Dungeons

Dungeons are generated **graph first, space second, voxels last**, so the
gameplay structure (pacing, keys, loops, boss) is guaranteed before any
geometry exists.

```
Mission Graph  →  Spatial Embedding (4 m cell grid)  →  Kit Assembly (voxel stamps)
               →  Dressing & Lighting  →  Encounters & Loot  →  Validation
```

> **Party-run additions** (see [10](10-party-and-dungeon-runs.md)): dungeons
> are 2–3 **wings or segments** with **3–4 boss slots**. Enemies are placed as
> **role-mixed packs** with `dread_value`. The graph emits **alternative
> routes**, and outdoor segments are generated by the overworld terrain
> generator inside a bounded cell corridor. In Charted mode the seed is fixed
> per week.

## 1. Mission graph
Nodes are *rooms with a purpose*, and edges are connections.

- **Node types:** `entrance`, `combat`, `elite`, `treasure`, `shrine`,
  `puzzle_lever`, `key`, `lock`, `stairs_down`, `boss_antechamber`, `boss`,
  `secret`.
- **Generation:** start from a **cyclic template** chosen per dungeon theme
  (inspired by cyclic dungeon generation), then rewrite it with rules:
  - *Main path* of N combat rooms from entrance to boss antechamber.
  - *Lock & key:* insert a `lock` on the main path and place a `key` on a
    side branch that loops back.
  - *Shortcut loop:* a one-way gate from near the boss back to the entrance,
    unlocked from the far side.
  - *Side branches:* 1–3 dead ends that end in `treasure` or `shrine`.
  - *Secret:* a 15% chance of a breakable-wall secret room.
- **Pacing tags** per node (`intensity 0–3`) shape the encounter budget:
  rising intensity with breathers.

## 2. Spatial embedding (cell grid)
- The world is divided into **4 m cells** (40 world voxels). A room takes
  1×1 to 6×6 cells; corridors are 1 cell wide.
- Rooms come from a **room shape library**: rectangle, L, cross, octagon
  (chapter house), long nave with side aisles (cathedral), circular (ossuary
  rotunda), cave blob (natural caverns).
- **Placement:** grow the graph breadth-first. Each new room tries K
  candidate positions or orientations next to its parent and checks overlap
  and bounds. If no candidate fits, backtrack that branch. Loops are closed
  with A\* corridors on the cell grid.
- **Verticality:** `stairs_down` nodes link to the next floor. Rooms can be
  sunken or raised by ±1 tier (balconies and pits) for visual interest while
  staying navigable.

## 3. Kit assembly (cells → voxels)
Each **dungeon theme** defines a *kit*: procedural stamp generators for each
cell edge and feature, all in meters.

| Kit piece | Gothic Crypt / Cathedral example |
|---|---|
| `floor` | Flagstones with randomized slab sizes, mortar lines, cracks, inlaid tomb slabs |
| `wall` | Ashlar blocks with 1-voxel mortar recess, buttress pilasters every 2 cells |
| `wall_window` | Pointed-arch lancet with stained glass (emissive, transparent) |
| `door` | Pointed-arch doorway with voussoirs; iron-banded oak door entity |
| `pillar` | Clustered column with base, shaft and foliate capital |
| `ceiling` | Rib vault: diagonal ribs as arcs + boss stone. Faded by the cutaway shader. |
| `stairs` | Spiral stair (tower) or straight flight with balustrade |
| `alcove` | Burial niche (loculus) with skull/bone fill |

Other themes reuse the system with different kits:
- **Catacombs:** bone-lined walls (skull mosaics), low barrel vaults, ossuary piles.
- **Sand Tomb** (desert): sandstone, hieroglyph-like relief bands (abstract
  glyph grammar), sand spill from cracks, sarcophagus pits.
- **Ice Caverns** (snow): cave-blob rooms, ice-crystal pillars,
  transparent ice walls with frozen corpses inside.
- **Castle Dungeon:** rough stone, iron cages, torture racks, drainage channels.

Cell edges between rooms and corridors pick the right piece (`door`, `arch`,
`portcullis`, `collapsed`) from the graph edge type.

## 4. Dressing & lighting
- **Dressing rules** per room purpose and theme: tables of props with
  placement anchors (`against_wall`, `center`, `corner`, `on_table`,
  `hanging`). For example, a crypt `combat` room gets 2–4 sarcophagi, candles
  in alcoves, cobwebs in ceiling corners, bones and loose stones.
- **Lighting rules:** each room has a light budget (1–4 lights). Prefer
  candelabras, braziers and wall sconces as the light sources, so every light
  has a visible origin. Keep some rooms deliberately dark, with only the
  player's lantern radius.
- **Story vignettes** (5% per room): a small authored arrangement, such as a
  dead adventurer with a journal, a ritual circle or a collapsed ceiling with
  moonlight.

## 5. Encounters & loot
- Each room's **pack budget** = `base × wing_mult × intensity` is spent on
  **role-mixed packs** (frontliner + ranged + caster/support) from the
  dungeon's enemy family. Every pack carries `dread_value`.
- **Dread tuning:** the sum of Dread across the *whole* dungeon is about
  130–150% of what's required, so groups choose a route. The validator checks
  that every alternative route can reach 100%.
- **Pull space:** each pack leaves a clear area of at least 1 cell between it
  and the next pack, so careful tanks can isolate pulls. Rare "gauntlet"
  rooms break this deliberately.
- Elites go in `elite` rooms. Bosses are fixed per dungeon slot (their
  arenas are generated, but their mechanics are authored).
- Chests, sarcophagi and weapon racks roll on loot tables. `treasure` rooms
  get a guaranteed rare.

## 6. Validation (always runs; failures trigger a reseed with `seed+1`)
- Every node is reachable on the navmesh from the entrance.
- Every key is reachable before its lock.
- The boss room fits its arena minimum (e.g. 6×6 cells for the Charnel Golem).
- No light-less combat room unless it is tagged `dark`.
- Golden-seed tests snapshot the graph and layout hashes.
