# 09 — First Level Layout

The first playable content is the **Morrowgate lobby** plus the first party
dungeon, the **Undercroft of St. Vael**. Its layout is procedural, but the
*structure* below is fixed by the dungeon recipe, so every seed plays like
this.

---

## Part A — Morrowgate (Lobby hub)

A walled Gothic town square at dusk beneath the broken silhouette of the
St. Vael cathedral, about 60 × 60 m. The layout is fixed so players learn it.
Buildings come from the Gothic grammar with a fixed seed, and dressing
(banners, crates, candles, crows) is reseeded weekly, so the town feels
alive without being disorienting.

```
                       N
             ┌───────────────────────────────┐
             │   ▲▲ CATHEDRAL RUIN (backdrop)│
             │  ╔═══════════════════════╗    │
             │  ║   RELIQUARY TABLE     ║    │   ← dungeon map: pick dungeon / Depth / mode
             │  ║  (chapel interior)    ║    │
             │  ╚══════════╤════════════╝    │
   ┌─────────┤ HALL OF     │      PARTY      ├─────────┐
   │BLACKSMITH│ RECORDS    │      BOARD      │ JEWELER │
   │ (forge)  │(leaderbds) │ (invite / AI    │ & ALCH. │
   └─────────┤             │  companions)    ├─────────┘
             │      ·  ·  ·│· FOUNTAIN · · · │
             │   STASH     ⊕    TRANSMOG     │
             │   VAULT          WARDROBE     │
             │ TRAINING YARD (dummy, mechanic│
             │  sandbox)          ░ graveyard│
             └──────────────┬────────────────┘
                     S gate → Weeping Moor (optional zone)
```

- **Spawn:** at the fountain (⊕). Party members appear around it.
- **Flow:** Party Board → gear check (vendors) → Reliquary Table → party
  teleports in.
- **Mood:** fog in the streets, warm candlelit windows (emissive voxels), a
  bell tolling at intervals, crows on rooftops that scatter when players run
  past.

## Part B — Undercroft of St. Vael (Dungeon 1)

- **Segments:** 3 (Nave → Crypts → Ossuary)
- **Bosses:** 3
- **Timer:** 28:00
- **Dread required:** 100%; ~140% is available
- **Enemy family:** Restless Dead
- **Intended length:** ~25 min at Depth 0

### Segment 1 — The Shattered Nave (cathedral interior, ~56 × 80 m)
Generated by the cathedral building grammar *as a dungeon segment*: nave,
side aisles, transepts, collapsed vault spans with moonlight shafts through
the holes.

```
            [APSE: BOSS 1 — Abbess Morwen]
                 ╭───────────╮
                 │  altar  ✝ │
          ┌──────┴───────────┴──────┐
  N.Trans │ ▓ pack   choir    pack ▓│ S.Transept → stairs ▼ (to Crypts, sealed until Boss 1 dies)
          ├──┬──┬──┬──┬──┬──┬──┬──┬─┤
  aisle   │▓ │  │░░│  │▓▓│  │  │░░│ │   ▓ = pack   ░ = collapsed rubble (blocks LOS, cover)
  NAVE    │  pew rows   pew rows    │   alt route: N aisle (3 small packs) vs
  aisle   │ ▓│  │  │▓▓│  │  │▓ │  │ │              nave center (1 elite pack, fewer Dread %)
          └───────────┤ ├───────────┘
                 WEST PORTAL (entry, sigil activation)
```
- **Packs:** skeleton warriors + archers, *Wax-faced Acolytes* (casters:
  **interrupt** their "Mend Bones" or the pack heals).
- **Boss 1: Abbess Morwen** (summoner, medium-size caster boss)
  - *Litany of Bones* `interrupt_or_wipe`: a 3 s channel, and an uninterrupted
    cast raises 6 skeletons.
  - *Pale Censure* `spread`: marks 2 players, who must separate or share
    heavy damage.
  - *Votive Candles* `soak`: candles spawn around the room, and unlit ones
    detonate after 10 s. Players light them by standing in them.

### Segment 2 — The Crypts (indoor dungeon generator, ~24 × 24 cells)
Classic graph-first crypt: corridors of burial niches, sarcophagus halls and
a lock-and-key loop.

```
 ▼ from Nave
 [E]──[C]──[C]──[LOCK: iron gate]──[C]──[Boss 2 antechamber]──[BOSS 2]
        │                              ▲
       [C]──[elite]──[KEY: Sexton's Key]┘ (loop back)
        │
       [treasure]          [secret? 15%: breakable wall → reliquary chest]
```
- **New enemies:** *Candle Wraiths* (flying, untargetable outside light
  radius, so the tank must pull them into candlelight), *Bone Brutes*
  (elite).
- **Boss 2: The Deacon of Wax** (large, slow, a melting wax giant)
  - *Molten Drip*: leaves wax pools that harden into walls after 6 s,
    permanently changing the arena. This is a voxel-native mechanic: the
    pools really are stamped into the arena's voxels.
  - *Wickflare* `tank_buster`: heavy fire hit on its tank.
  - At 30% HP it melts into 3 small Wax Effigies `adds_phase` that must die
    within 5 s of each other.

### Segment 3 — The Ossuary (catacombs kit, ~16 × 16 cells)
Bone-lined tunnels descending to the **octagonal chapter house**, the
Charnel Golem's arena (6×6 cells, 8 breakable pillars).

```
 [from Crypts] → [bone gallery: gauntlet of 3 packs, no pull space] → [ossuary rotunda (shrine: party buff)]
      → [descending stair] → [CHAPTER HOUSE — BOSS 3: Charnel Golem]
                                   ╱‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾╲
                                  │ ◯    ◯ │  ◯ = fragile pillars
                                  │◯  ⚔   ◯│  (charge into 2 → ceiling collapse,
                                  │ ◯    ◯ │   bonus damage on boss)
                                   ╲________╱
```
- **Boss 3: Charnel Golem**: full moveset in [07-enemies.md](07-enemies.md).
- **End chest** spawns on the altar after the kill. Its quality depends on
  Depth and the timer result, and the sigil upgrades if the run was in
  time.

## Part C — Optional: The Weeping Moor (overworld side zone)
Reached from Morrowgate's south gate. A 256 × 256 m procedural moor (see
[03](03-overworld-generation.md)) with the old graveyard, burned farmsteads,
lore stones, roaming skeleton packs, and the **Hollow Rider** world boss at
the gallows crossroads at night. It is the story intro for new characters
and drops the first **Reliquary Sigils**. After that, players can skip it
entirely.
